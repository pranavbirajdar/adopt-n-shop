About Our Mission

Connecting Loving Homes with Rescue Animals

Adopt n Shop was created as a final-year BCA project to modernize shelter operations and pet retail into an integrated, transparent platform.

Every year, millions of companion animals end up in shelters. Our goal is to streamline the adoption process with transparent applications, digital tracking, and integrated pet care e-commerce to ensure every newly adopted pet receives the nutrition and supplies they need right from day one.
